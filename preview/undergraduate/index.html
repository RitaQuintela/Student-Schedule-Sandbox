Press “+ Add” on a course, or drag it onto the timetable, to add it; a course with several sections asks for its section first. Point at a section to try it on the timetable before you choose. To remove a course, use its × or drag a class back to the course list. Red means two classes happen at the same time.

Schedule Sandbox is a beta planning tool: it does not enroll you in anything. Use it to plan your enrollment on the official platforms, and always check the information against the official class timetable and exam calendar.
